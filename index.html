3. Who We Are

Hureeyerhs Limited is a diversified Nigerian company operating across interior design and custom furniture, travel and tourism, fragrances and lifestyle products, and beauty and personal care services. Through our specialised business divisions, we provide innovative solutions, quality products, and professional services tailored to the needs of individuals, businesses, and organisations.

We are driven by creativity, excellence, integrity, and a commitment to customer satisfaction. Our goal is to build a trusted and sustainable business that delivers exceptional value, creates employment opportunities, and contributes to economic growth while expanding our reach across Nigeria and international markets.

Our mission is to provide innovative, high-quality, and customer-focused solutions across our business divisions, including interior design, furniture production, travel services, fragrances, and beauty care. 1. What Do You Sell?

Scents by Hureeyerh is a fragrance and lifestyle brand specialising in quality perfumes, luxury bakhoor, traditional incense, perfume oils, and home fragrance products. We offer a diverse collection of scents designed to enhance personal grooming, create inviting environments, and bring elegance and a memorable fragrance experience to everyday life.

Our product range includes:

  • Luxury Bakhoor and Incense: Premium bakhoor, traditional incense, and specially selected fragrance blends for homes, special occasions, and cultural gatherings.
  • Perfumes: Personal fragrances for men and women, suitable for everyday use and special occasions.
  • Perfume Oils: Concentrated fragrance oils for customers who appreciate distinctive and rich scents.
  • Khumrah: Fragrant blends designed to complement personal fragrance routines and traditional beauty practices.
  • Home Fragrances: Room sprays, air fresheners, reed diffusers, and other products that create pleasant indoor environments.
  • Scented Candles: Fragrant candles for homes, relaxation, and decorative purposes.
  • Car Fragrances: Products designed to keep vehicles smelling fresh and pleasant.
  • Fragrance Accessories: Scent burners and other accessories used to enjoy and display fragrance products.
  • Wholesale and Retail Supply: Fragrance products available to individual customers, retailers, resellers, and bulk buyers.

Our goal is to make quality fragrances accessible to a wide range of customers while offering products that suit different preferences, lifestyles, and budgets.
